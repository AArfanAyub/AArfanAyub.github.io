Scope

What it does

A till is easy. A till that also keeps honest books is not.

🛒Sales

Fast cart entry with barcode and SKU lookup, per-line and order discounts with reason codes, split and multi-tender payments, held carts and receipts. Every committed sale is immutable — corrections are made by reversal, so the record of what actually happened survives.

📦Inventory

Stock is tracked through a movement ledger rather than a single editable number, so the current quantity is always explainable. Receiving with cost, stocktake variance, low-stock thresholds, and optional negative-stock prevention.

📒Accounting

A real double-entry ledger sits underneath the till: chart of accounts, journal entries, general ledger, income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and trial balance. Every sale, void and refund posts automatically.

🧮Shifts

Shift open and close with an opening float and cash movements, X and Z reports, and cash reconciliation against expected drawer totals. Exports to CSV, Excel and PDF.

🧾E-invoicing

Tax invoice records with customer details, submission tracking and cancellation, with the submission credentials configurable in Settings.

💳Tenders

Cash, e-wallet QR, and an external card terminal that is recorded, not verified — the software does not pretend to know what the bank approved.

The part that matters

Balanced by construction

The books cannot silently drift out of balance, because the database will not accept it.

Most retail software bolts accounting on afterwards: a sale is a row in a sales table, and a month-end export is reconciled by hand into an accounting package. That is where small businesses lose their books.

In Serambi a sale is a business event that posts a journal entry. Debits always equal credits — enforced at the database level, not by the application remembering to do it. Posted entries cannot be edited or deleted; they are reversed by a counter-entry. The trial balance therefore balances because it must, not because someone checked.

Install

Five minutes, one machine

  1. Download Serambi-POS-Pro-Setup-3.0.0.exe from the releases page.
  2. Double-click it. Windows SmartScreen will warn you because the installer is not code-signed — choose More info → Run anyway.
  3. It installs to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Serambi POS Pro and adds a desktop shortcut.
  4. Launch it and complete first-run setup: outlet details, tax code, owner account.
  5. Activate with your licence key, or use the 14-day trial.

Back up by copying %APPDATA%\serambi-pos-pro\. That folder is the whole business.

Straight answers

Four things worth knowing

⚠️The installer is unsigned

Code-signing certificates cost money annually. Until one is purchased, SmartScreen will warn on first run. The warning is expected and does not indicate a problem with the file.

🔄There is no auto-update

Download a newer installer when one is released. Your data is preserved across upgrades. Nothing reaches out to a server on its own.

🔒The source is not published

Serambi POS Pro is proprietary. This is a release repository, not a source repository. Third-party components and their licences are listed in the notices file.
